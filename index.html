Who We Are

Carriers of the Gospel, Not Performers of Religion

We are not a denomination; we are demonstrators, going beyond the four walls to reveal Christ in action.

We are not partners of the Gospel — we are carriers of it; not performers of religion, but living prototypes of Jesus Christ, walking in His love, authority, and compassion.

As a global mandate raising and equipping a radical army to win souls, we believe the Gospel must be demonstrated, not just preached — through every outreach, act of kindness, and expression of truth.

We go beyond the four walls to reveal Christ in action — feeding the hungry, clothing the poor, healing the sick, and reaching the forgotten. This is more than a ministry; it is a movement — revival in motion, calling believers to walk in power, humility, and service as we advance the Gospel and transform lives across nations.

What We Stand On

Demonstration, Not Performance

  • We demonstrate the Gospel through outreach, acts of kindness, and expressions of truth — not performance.
  • We go beyond the four walls of the church to reveal Christ in action, everywhere people are.
  • We feed the hungry, clothe the poor, heal the sick, and reach the forgotten.
  • We call believers to walk in power, humility, and service as living prototypes of Jesus Christ.

What We Do

Revival in Motion — Across Every Front

We unify training, missions, prayer, evangelism, media, and discipleship into one coordinated movement advancing the Gospel across nations.

01

Global Training & Dispatch Hub

We train, disciple, and equip believers and ministers, then deploy them as a global army to advance the Gospel across nations.

02

Missions & Evangelism Movement

We operate actively throughout the U.S., Africa, Asia, and beyond — mobilizing missionaries and galvanizing leaders to reach regions through strategic, Spirit-led evangelism.

03

House of Prayer (Prayer Hubs)

Our flagship ministry serves as a global prayer command center — raising prayer warriors and leaders who intercede daily and weekly for regions, souls, and divine direction. These hubs are being established in cities worldwide.

04

Street & Community Evangelism

Our evangelism teams are the heartbeat of the movement — trained to reach people in streets, neighborhoods, schools, workplaces, and rural areas through outreach, prayer, and Holy Spirit-led encounters.

05

Revival Events & Crusades

We host citywide revivals, crusades, and outreach gatherings marked by worship, preaching, healing, deliverance, and mass salvation — awakening hearts and calling people back to God.

06

Media & Digital Gospel Expansion

We spread the message of Christ through social media, podcasts, video, and digital platforms — sharing testimonies, teachings, and live encounters to reach souls globally.

07

Youth4ChristNow

We raise bold, unashamed young leaders through mentorship, training, school outreach, and creative expression — equipping the next generation to stand and lead for Christ.

08

Global Partnership Network

We unify churches, ministries, and believers around the world to collaborate in prayer, missions, outreach, and resources to accelerate global soul-winning.

09

Discipleship & Follow-Up

We ensure every soul is nurtured — providing structured discipleship, mentorship, and biblical teaching so new believers grow, mature, and walk in their God-given authority.
